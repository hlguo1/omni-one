# Reply Playbook — 20 copy-paste templates + refund decision tree

> Speed + warmth + proof. Every draft the close generates already contains
> its `[file:line]` citation — keep it in when you send (customers trust
> receipts). Personalise **one** sentence; send **within 2 hours** for P0s.
>
> Priority tags in `replies.txt`: `P0-reply-in-2h` (refund / hasn't-arrived /
> damaged / chargeback words) vs `P1-today` (everything else).
>
> i18n pointers at the bottom: the same 20 templates work in any language —
> only the opener and the promise need translating (section 7).

## Refund decision tree (read this first)

```
Customer unhappy?
├─ Item never arrived + tracking dead > 5 days?
│   └─ YES → RESHIP now + refund shipping. Template 03. (Cost: ~1 AOV. Saves the review.)
├─ Item arrived damaged/defective + photo?
│   └─ YES → Offer choice: free replacement OR full refund. Template 05/06. (Choice defuses anger.)
├─ "Where is my order?" + tracking alive?
│   └─ YES → Tracking link + new ETA + 10% coupon. Template 02. (Most cases end here.)
├─ Demands refund, item OK, within your return window?
│   └─ YES → Accept, prepaid label if cheap. Template 09. (Fighting it costs more in fees + time.)
├─ Demands refund, outside window / worn / buyer's remorse + open threat (chargeback/1-star)?
│   └─ MAYBE → Partial refund (30-50%) as goodwill. Template 10. (Cheaper than a chargeback fee.)
└─ Abusive / fraud signals (3rd "never arrived" this month, empty photo, address games)?
    └─ YES → Polite firm decline + evidence. Template 12. (Document everything; eat the review if you must.)
```

**Money rule:** one AOV refund costs less than one 1-star review (which
suppresses conversion for months) and far less than a chargeback
($15–25 fee + the refund anyway). When in doubt, refund.

## Late / missing delivery (templates 01–04)

**01 — Late, tracking alive (P1)**
> Hi {name}! Thanks for your patience — I just pulled your order
> ({citation}) and tracking shows it arriving by {date}. I'll watch it
> personally and message you the moment it lands. — {shop}

**02 — No tracking yet (P0)**
> Hi {name}! I'm really sorry about your missing tracking — I just pulled
> your order ({citation}) and I'm checking with the carrier right now.
> I'll update you within 2 hours with tracking + a new ETA (and a fix if
> it's stuck: reship/refund — your choice). Thank you for your patience
> with {shop}!

**03 — Never arrived, tracking dead (P0)**
> Hi {name}! You shouldn't have to chase this — I'm reshipping your
> {product} today with tracked shipping, and refunding your original
> shipping (${x}). New tracking lands in your inbox tonight. ({citation})
> Truly sorry for the wait. — {shop}

**04 — Delivered-but-missing (P0)**
> Hi {name}! I see the carrier marked it delivered but you don't have it
> ({citation}) — this happens with mis-scans. I've opened a carrier trace
> and I'm preparing a reship in parallel; if the parcel doesn't surface in
> 48h, the replacement ships automatically. I'll confirm by {date}. — {shop}

## Damaged / wrong item (05–08)

**05 — Damaged on arrival (P0)**
> Thank you for flagging this ({citation}) — we're sorry about your
> {product} experience. We've fixed the packing check that caused it.
> Please DM us for a free replacement or refund — we want to make this
> right. — {shop}

**06 — Wrong item received (P0)**
> Hi {name}! That's our mistake — wrong item in your parcel ({citation}).
> Keep it (or gift it!) and I'll ship the correct {product} today; tracking
> tonight. If you'd rather refund, say the word. Sorry for the mix-up! — {shop}

**07 — Quality complaint, no photo yet (P1)**
> Hi {name}, I'm sorry the {product} didn't meet expectations ({citation}).
> Could you send one quick photo? I'll sort a replacement or refund the
> moment I see it — no return needed for small items. — {shop}

**08 — Bad review just landed (P0, public reply)**
> Thank you for flagging this ({citation}) — we're sorry about your
> {product} experience. We've fixed the packing/quality check that caused
> it. Please DM us for a free replacement or refund — we want to make this
> right. — {shop}
>
> Then DM them template 05 privately within the hour. Never argue in public.

## Refunds & returns (09–12)

**09 — Accept return in-window (P1)**
> Hi {name}! Of course — happy to take the {product} back ({citation}).
> Here's your prepaid label: {link}. Refund lands 2–3 days after the
> carrier scans it. Anything I can get right next time? — {shop}

**10 — Goodwill partial refund (P1)**
> Hi {name}, I can't do a full refund outside the window, but I'd like to
> make it fair: I'm refunding {x}% (${y}) now ({citation}), no return
> needed. If anything else comes up, message me directly. — {shop}

**11 — Refund issued confirmation (P1)**
> Hi {name}! Your refund of ${x} for {product} just went through
> ({citation}) — it shows in 3–5 business days depending on your bank.
> Sorry we didn't nail it this time; your next order ships with 15% off:
> {code}. — {shop}

**12 — Firm decline, suspected abuse (P1, manager tone)**
> Hi {name}, I've reviewed your order history ({citation}) and I can't
> approve another refund on this one. What I can do: {one concrete offer —
> discount code / free shipping next time}. I hope you'll give us another
> shot. — {shop}

## Operational (17–20)

**17 — Address change (caught in time)**
> Hi {name}! Changed the address to {new} ({citation}) — caught it before
> packing. Reply YES to confirm and I'll send tracking tonight. — {shop}

**18 — Address change (too late)**
> Hi {name}, your parcel already left for {old} ({citation}) — I'm sorry, I
> can't reroute it mid-flight. Here's what I'll do: if it bounces back,
> reship is free; if it delivers, I'll refund shipping + give you 15% off a
> re-order. — {shop}

**19 — Pre-order / made-to-order ETA**
> Hi {name}! Your {product} is made to order ({citation}) — current queue
> is {n} days, so expect shipping around {date}. I'll message tracking the
> day it leaves. Thanks for supporting handmade! — {shop}

**20 — Fee-transparent price note (when raising prices)**
> Hi! Quick note: channel fees just rose, so {product} moves from ${old} to
> ${new} on {date}. Orders before then keep the old price — grab it now if
> you've been eyeing it! — {shop}

## Timing SLA

| Priority | First reply | Resolution target | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 (refund/damaged/never-arrived/chargeback words) | < 2h, 7 days/week | 24h | you, personally |
| P1 (questions, address, praise) | same day | 48h | you or helper |
| Reviews ≤ 2★ | public reply < 24h + private DM < 1h | 72h | you |

Batching trick: two 15-minute inbox blocks daily (morning close + evening)
plus P0 push notifications. Nothing P0 survives the evening block.

## Tone rules (5)
1. Name them. Never "Dear customer".
2. One apology, then action. Never three apologies and no plan.
3. Give a time ("within 2 hours", "tonight", "by Friday"), then beat it.
4. Offer the *choice* (reship/refund) — choice restores control.
5. Keep the citation. Delete everything else before sending if you must, but keep proof.

## i18n pointers
- Templates 01–04, 08: translate opener + time-promise first (these carry the SLA).
- German/French/Japanese buyers: keep it formal (Sie/vous/です) until they go casual.
- Spanish/Portuguese: "reembolso / reenvío" must appear verbatim — buyers search for the word.
- CJK + emoji names: never mangle them (copy-paste exactly; the close preserves them byte-identical — see property tests).
- Right-to-left (Arabic/Hebrew): put the citation on its own line to avoid bidi breakage.
- Machine-translate the *template*, hand-fix the *promise sentence*. A wrong date in the right language still loses the customer.
