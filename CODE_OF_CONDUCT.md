# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Omni-One a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, or experience level.

## Our Standards

- Be welcoming, patient, and constructive.
- No harassment, trolling, insults, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Respect differing viewpoints; focus on what is best for users (small sellers, local-first).
- Cite evidence; disagree with ideas, not people.

## Enforcement

Instances may be reported by opening a GitHub issue titled `[CoC] <summary>` or emailing the maintainers via the repo profile. Maintainers will review and may remove, edit, or reject contributions, or temporarily/permanently ban contributors for violations.
